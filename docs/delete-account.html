Delete your account and data

In the app (fastest): Settings → Cloud account → Delete account. Your account and all cloud data (trades, settings, alerts, push tokens, error reports) are deleted immediately and permanently.
Without the app: email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my TradeMind account". We delete your account and all associated data within 7 days and confirm by email.

Trades stored only on your phone are not affected - uninstall the app or clear its data to remove them. Google Play keeps its own purchase records; cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
