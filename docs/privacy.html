Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This policy explains how TradeMind ICT ("the app"), provided by [YOUR NAME], handles your information.

What we collect

  • Without an account: everything you enter (trades, notes, settings, screenshots) stays on your phone. We receive nothing.
  • With a cloud account: your email address, and the journal data you choose to sync: trades, notes, checklist answers, account balance and risk settings, alert decisions, and scanner alerts you send from your own PC.
  • TradeMind Desktop (optional PC companion): if you use it with your account, it uploads your closed MetaTrader 5 trades and its scanner alerts to your cloud journal. If you turn on TradingView alerts, we store a secret webhook token for your account and the alert messages TradingView sends (symbol, buy/sell/close, stop, target), kept for at most 30 days. Your broker password never leaves your PC. Trade charts are saved on your own PC, not in the cloud.
  • Push notifications: a device push token, used only to deliver your own alerts.
  • Error reports: if the app crashes while you are signed in, a technical error message and the app version.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or M-Pesa details. Our subscription provider (RevenueCat) receives an anonymous purchase identifier linked to your account.

The economic calendar is downloaded through our server without any personal data. The trade coach analyses your journal on your phone only. Chart screenshots are not uploaded. We do not collect your location, contacts or your broker password.

How we use it

Only to provide the app's features: syncing your journal between your devices, delivering your alerts, managing your subscription and fixing errors. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Cloud data is stored with Supabase (database and login) in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules ensure each account can only access its own records.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your cloud data immediately. Backups held by our provider, if any, expire within 7 days.

Your rights

You can view, export (Settings → Back up / Export CSV), correct or delete your data at any time, and delete your account in the app (Settings → Cloud account → Delete account) or by request. Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may also object to processing or complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
